01 About

Useful things, well made.

I like turning everyday problems into clear, fast, well-designed products — the kind people can open on a phone and use right away.

I’m a Computer Science student from Pakistan, currently studying FSc Pre-Engineering with Computer Science. My background pairs graphic design with web development, so I care as much about how a product looks and feels as I do about how it works.

My recent work covers business websites, an online hardware store, browser-based file tools, and AI-assisted civic technology that lets people report public problems by photo, voice or text in Urdu or English.

I’m looking for freelance web projects, collaborations on AI and software ideas, internship opportunities, and people who enjoy building things that genuinely help.
